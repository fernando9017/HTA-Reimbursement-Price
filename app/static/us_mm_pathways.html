Overview

Clinical pathways are evidence-based treatment decision tools that recommend standardised regimens based on efficacy, toxicity, and cost. In the US, roughly 64% of oncologists are exposed to third-party clinical pathways, and ~86% adherence is reported among those subject to them. Multiple Myeloma is a key haematology/oncology tumour type targeted by pathway programs.

This module provides an asset-specific view of the US Multiple Myeloma pathway landscape, centred on Elrexfio (elranatamab) — a BCMA-directed bispecific antibody. It tracks pathway positioning across payer-driven and provider/institution-driven programs, with dedicated competitive intelligence for the bispecific and BCMA-targeted therapy classes.

Elrexfio (elranatamab-bcmm) — Product Overview

Elrexfio is a BCMA × CD3 bispecific T-cell engager approved for the treatment of relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma. This section summarises the key product attributes, approved indications, and regulatory milestones relevant to pathway positioning.

Competitive Landscape — BCMA-Targeted & Bispecific Therapies

Elrexfio competes in the BCMA-targeted therapy class alongside CAR-T therapies (idecabtagene vicleucel, ciltacabtagene autoleucel), other bispecific antibodies (teclistamab), and antibody-drug conjugates (belantamab mafodotin). This section maps the competitive positioning across pathway programs.
